**Hierarchy** is a common concept: organization of things based on some being higher-up than others. For example, taxonomies: animal, mammal, feline, house cat. In mathematics, all manner of partial orders.

**Heterarchy**: the organization of things according to their differences, or more generally their interrelationships. For example, as used in economics: Rabbits are the things that Foxes eat. Foxes are the things that eat rabbits. From this concept, arises partial differential systems and chaos theory.

**Homoarchy**: the organization of things according to their relationship with themselves. Concepts here include narcissism: someone who loves themselves; depression: (one view of this) inability to feel one's feelings; also, self-loathing, and masturbation. From this view arises disciplines like self-development, psychoanalysis. In mathematics, the theory of fixed points, and recursion.
