We know a fractal as an object that has the amazing property that by looking at a smaller piece of it, you can still understand and see the whole. Each 'piece' contains within it a replica of the complete object's pattern.

So what's a co-fractal? The opposite of a fractal! An object that you cannot understand until you look at every part of it! Its meaning will escape unless you consider and see every part at once. Every part of it is equally unique, special and unmissable.
