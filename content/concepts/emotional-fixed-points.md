One day, I was walking down the street feeling very grumpy. I was aware of how grumpy I was, and realising how grumpy I was I felt even more grumpy about it. Then it hit me. Awareness applied to grumpiness results in ... grumpiness, and it reminded me of a very cool thing in mathematics. The concept of fixed point, which you write like this: `f(x) = x`, and say that `x` is a fixed-point for the function `f`. Let `f` = Awareness, and `x` = grumpiness, and then one sees that I had an emotional fixed point for awareness. This made me very happy to see!

I learned of an attractive fixed point: this is where repeated applications of a function result in a fixed point `f(... f(x) ...) = x`. Of course, the funny thing is that when my attention shifted to thinking about mathematics, I became very happy, so `f(f(x))` is not equal to `x`... I wonder, what's an example of an emotionally attractive fixed point? Does it exist? Maybe awareness of awareness?
