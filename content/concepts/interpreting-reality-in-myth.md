Your own real life can be viewed through the lens of myths. Think about your own reactions to something — be it a fight with a friend, or a smile from a stranger — you can ask yourself: what's the quality and dynamic here in me? What myths does that remind me of?

It's a bridge to the imaginal, and from that place of lower pressure we can ask what the fundamental things that are happening really are: it's easier to see reality when you step outside it and look at it from the mythical.
