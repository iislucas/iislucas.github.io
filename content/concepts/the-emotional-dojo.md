Every week you meet with 6 others for emotion training. You agree not to see each other outside of this time. The group sessions follow these principles:

* Say everything you feel and think
* Take your share of time (not your equal share, how much feels right, you have to ask yourself. Also this is impossible if you try and do (1)... life isn't always easy)
* Say how you feel and why, and in particular: express your feelings towards the object of your attention (yes! your attention has an object, and how you feel depends on that)
* Tell the emotionally meaningful story of your life (the stuff that means something to you — the stuff that you feel something to tell, whatever that is, whether it's your love for stamp collecting, or how the anger you had toward that taxi driver...).

After a few weeks, you start to notice when people talk without saying how they feel. And you realise you want to know what they feel. After a few more weeks, you start to notice that you like saying how you feel towards things; it's thrilling and liberating, and often quite scary.
