Human development progresses in part by projection onto others of what we have to grow and develop. Inner Gold describes the quality of admiration, also the tingling magic of falling in love, as a projection of a quality that is for us to develop.

Our having been able to project this quality onto someone else is a key step in accepting and integrating the quality. The experience is a key clue that there is something for us there. It is then for us to work out what that quality is, and how we can bring that into our life (getting our inner Gold back!).

Contrary to popular culture, it says nothing about what kind of relationship we should have with the person we project onto, how long it would last should we develop more of a relation with them, or how we should act towards them. The feeling is a thing to observe and reflect on for our own development.
