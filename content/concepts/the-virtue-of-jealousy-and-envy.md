Jealousy and Envy are often thought of as negative and bad emotions, but one can also see them as critical forces for a benevolent society too. They help keep societies from becoming abusively autocratic.

We feel them when something is out of balance; when someone has something that we should have some form of (it's not that we should have the thing another has exactly, but some aspect of it). Extreme inequality is something we should feel angry about, we should suffer when we see it. Without it, gross abuse of power would lack an emotional backlash to bring back a balance to society.

So here's a toast to Jealousy and Envy!
