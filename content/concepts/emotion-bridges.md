There are pairs of twin-emotions. Such pairs have a similar texture, and one can often transform one of the twins into the other by placing some attention and making some emotional effort. I call such effort an "emotional coordination", and the act of transformation an "emotional bridge". Here are some examples:

* **Nervousness and Excitement**. Nervousness, for example before giving a talk or performance, can sometimes be transformed into excitement and energy about giving the talk. Similarly, a feeling of excitement about an event can easily turn into fear: what if it goes totally wrong?
* **Jealousy and Compersion**: your partner's sexual experiences with someone else (consider the whole spectrum from mild attraction to full on lust), can lead you to feel jealous, but it can also lead to compersion (the positive non-sexual feeling of happiness towards a partner's sexual feelings towards someone else). At different points along the intensity, we tend to be pulled at different strengths towards either jealousy or compersion. At times we can find we have a choice which way the feeling goes simply by paying attention.
